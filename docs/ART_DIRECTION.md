# ASHFALL — Art Direction

## Rule

Gameplay first. Placeholder geometry is acceptable and expected. Art must never
block simulation development.

Player, tree, house, monster, weapon — all primitives until the loop is fun.

## Eventual identity

Readable, cohesive, atmospheric, recognizable, performant, scalable. Top-down /
angled overworld that communicates geography the way RollerCoaster Tycoon, Age
of Empires and Cossacks do — without being an RTS.

## Interface palette (current, defined in `src/styles.css`)

Ash and ember: cold grey-blue ash for surfaces, a hot ember accent for anything
live or dangerous, bone-white text. No purple. No gradient-on-white.

## Readability rules

- Silhouette before detail.
- Danger reads at a glance.
- Terrain type must be distinguishable without colour alone.
